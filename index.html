    How the hell does this work?

    Proper science, mate. Every hour we run the Penman-Monteith evaporation equation (the one meteorologists use for how fast water leaves wet surfaces) on your washing: sunshine heating it, how thirsty the air is, wind carrying the vapour away, and heat lost to a clear night sky, which can even put dew back on it. Thicker stuff holds more water and lets go of it more slowly. Rain re-wets the load. We add it all up from the moment you peg it out until it hits 100%. It's a bloody good guess, not a promise. Forecasts come from Open-Meteo. In the UK and Ireland it uses the Met Office forecast.
